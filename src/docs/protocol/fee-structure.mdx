# Fee Structure

Minke charges a protocol fee when VT holders stake in the RedeemPool and receive the underlying token (T) as it vests. There is no upfront deposit fee and no fee on redemption at maturity.

## When the Fee Applies

The protocol fee (e.g., 5%) is deducted at the moment the RedeemPool distributes T to stakers. It is not a separate action — it is embedded in the staking/claiming process:

```
RedeemPool pulls 100 T from Vault
  → 95 T distributed to stakers (e.g., protocolFeeRate = 50/1000 = 5%)
  →  5 T → protocol treasury
```

Each staker receives their proportional share of the 95 T, based on their share of the pool at the time of the pull.

## What the Fee Is NOT

- **Not an upfront deposit fee** — depositing T into the vault has no fee
- **Not a VT minting fee** — minting VT has no fee
- **Not a Maturity redemption fee** — `redeemVT()` at maturity is always 1:1 with no fee

## Fee Rate

The fee rate is stored as `protocolFeeRate` in basis points out of 1000:

```
protocolFeeRate = 50   →  5% fee  →  95% to stakers
protocolFeeRate = 100  →  10% fee →  90% to stakers
```

The default rate is **50 (5%)**. The Protocol Owner can update the rate on a per-pool basis, up to a maximum of **200 (20%)**.

Users can verify the current rate by reading `protocolFeeRate` on the deployed RedeemPool contract.

<Callout type="note">
The protocol fee rate is configurable by the Protocol Owner, up to a maximum of 20%. Any updates apply going forward — already-distributed T is not affected.
</Callout>
