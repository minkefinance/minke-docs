# SAFTs and Team Vesting

Simple Agreements for Future Tokens (SAFTs) and team/advisor vesting allocations are the highest-concentration DFI category in crypto — and the most natural fit for Minke.

## The Status Quo

Most token launches allocate 15–40% of total supply to team, advisors, and investors, vesting over 2–4 years with a 12-month cliff. This creates a structural problem:

- **Teams** are locked in for years with significant personal wealth concentrated in one illiquid asset
- **Investors** who want early returns must negotiate OTC deals at 40–80% discounts with trusted counterparties
- **Protocols** face ongoing selling pressure as vesting unlocks create market overhangs

None of this is necessary. Minke provides an alternative.

## How Minke Solves It

A recipient with a vesting allocation (advisor, investor, team member) can:

1. Receive VT from the issuer (minted directly to their wallet via `mintVT`)
2. Sell VTs on a secondary market at a time discount — getting liquidity today without breaking vesting terms
3. The buyer takes on the time risk and earns T via RedeemPool or holds to Maturity

<Callout type="tip" title="Vesting terms preserved">
The underlying tokens remain in the vault on the original vesting schedule. From the protocol's perspective, nothing changes. Only the economic claim has been tokenized and made liquid.
</Callout>

## Example Flow

Suppose an advisor holds 500,000 TOKEN vesting linearly over 24 months:

```
── ADVISOR (originator) ───────────────────────────────────────
Setup:  Project creates Standard Pool (24-month linear),
        deposits 500,000 TOKEN into vault
        Issuer calls mintVT(advisor, 500,000) → advisor receives 500,000 VT

Goal:   Access value today without waiting 2 years
Action: Sells 500,000 VT on AMM at 0.82 T per VT
Result: Receives ~410,000 TOKEN today ✓
        (Cost of liquidity: 18% discount on par)

── BUYER (investor) ───────────────────────────────────────────
Buys:   500,000 VT at 0.82 T per VT → pays ~410,000 TOKEN

Option A: Stake in RedeemPool
          → Receives TOKEN gradually over 24 months as vault releases
          → e.g. ~19,792 TOKEN/month × 0.95 = ~18,802 TOKEN/month (after fee)
          → Total ~451,250 TOKEN over 24 months (vs 410,000 TOKEN paid)

Option B: Hold to Maturity
          → vestingEnded = true at Month 24
          → redeemVT(500,000 VT) → 500,000 TOKEN (1:1, no fee)
          → Earns the full 18% discount as profit
```

## SAFT-Specific Considerations

SAFTs are typically larger ($1M–$100M) and have more complex vesting terms (cliff + linear, multiple tranches). Minke handles these via:

| SAFT Feature | Minke Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Cliff period | DFIOracle PIECEWISE schedule — 0% release until cliff date |
| Linear unlock | DFIOracle LINEAR or piecewise ramp after cliff |
| Multiple tranches | Deploy multiple Standard Pools, one per tranche |
| Token Generation Event | Deposit occurs at TGE; schedule starts from that timestamp |

## Benefits for Protocols

Protocols that adopt Minke for their vesting infrastructure gain:

- **Reduced selling pressure** — recipients get liquidity via VT sales rather than spot token sales
- **Alignment signaling** — the underlying still vests; recipients haven't exited their long-term position
- **Institutional-grade tooling** — large funds and family offices can manage SAFT exposure through a standardised on-chain instrument

<Callout type="info">
Several protocols are exploring Minke-native vesting launches, where VTs are distributed directly to investors at TGE, removing the illiquidity friction from the outset.
</Callout>
