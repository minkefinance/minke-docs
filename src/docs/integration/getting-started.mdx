# Getting Started

Navigate to `/dashboard` (the **Projects** link in the top nav) and connect your wallet.

After connecting you will see two sections:

- **Oracles** — DFI Oracles you have deployed, with their approval status badge.
- **Vaults / Pools** — Vesting pools already live on-chain.

Click **Setup Oracle** to begin — this is the entry point for both Standard Pool and Custom Pool.

From here the creation flow has two stages:

1. [Create Oracle](/integration/create-oracle) — deploy a DFI Oracle and get it approved by the Minke team.
2. [Create Vault](/integration/create-vault) — launch the on-chain vault linked to your approved oracle.

Once the vault is live, continue to [Standard Pool](/integration/standard-pool) or [Custom Pool](/integration/custom-pool) for day-to-day pool management.
